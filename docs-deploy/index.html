🏸 Squash Club Booking & Management System

A complete, production-ready WordPress plugin built exclusively for squash clubs. It replaces fragmented booking forms with one integrated platform covering Sunday Clinics, Saturday Private Lessons, Summer Camp Registrations, automated waitlists, WooCommerce payments, and branded email notifications — all from a modern custom admin dashboard.

✅ WordPress 6.0+ ✅ PHP 7.4+ ✅ WooCommerce Required ✅ 12 Custom DB Tables ✅ Mobile Responsive

What This Plugin Does

At a high level, the plugin provides three booking services for your club members:

  • Sunday Squash Clinics – Junior & Adult group sessions every Sunday with per-slot capacity, waitlist, and automatic slot generation.
  • Saturday Private & Semi-Private Lessons – 1-on-1 or 2-player coaching sessions with real-time slot availability per coach.
  • Summer Camp Registration – Multi-week youth academies with age validation, parent info, and payment deadlines.

All bookings go through WooCommerce checkout, and customers receive branded confirmation emails automatically.

📦 Installation Guide

Method 1: Manual Upload (Recommended)

  1. 1

    Upload the plugin folder

    Copy the squash-club-booking folder into wp-content/plugins/ on your server via FTP, cPanel File Manager, or SFTP.

  2. 2

    Activate WooCommerce first

    Go to WP Admin → Plugins and ensure WooCommerce is installed and activated before proceeding.

  3. 3

    Activate the plugin

    Find Squash Club Booking & Management System in the plugins list and click Activate.

  4. 4

    Automatic database setup

    Upon activation, the plugin automatically creates 12 custom database tables and seeds them with 9 coaches, 4 weeks of Sunday clinic slots, and 3 summer camp programs.

  5. 5

    Verify the admin menu

    A new Squash Club menu item will appear in the WP Admin sidebar — your dedicated management dashboard.

✅

The plugin comes pre-seeded with realistic sample data. You can clear and replace it via Squash Club → Settings → Reset Tables at any time.

🔧 First-Time Setup

After activation, follow these steps to configure the plugin for your club:

Step 1 – Configure Club Settings

Go to Squash Club → Settings and fill in:

  • Club Name – Used in emails and booking confirmations.
  • Admin Email – Receives alerts for new bookings and camp registrations.
  • Currency – Default is USD. Change to your local currency.
  • Timezone – Should match your WordPress timezone setting.
  • Lesson Pricing – Set your private lesson rate (default $80) and semi-private rate (default $40).
  • Clinic Pricing – Junior rate (default $25) and Adult rate (default $30).

Step 2 – Create Frontend Pages

Navigate to Squash Club → Settings → Shortcode Pages and click "Create Pages Automatically". This creates 5 WordPress pages with the correct shortcodes embedded:

Page NameSlugShortcode
Sunday Squash Clinics/sunday-clinics[scb_sunday_clinic]
Private Lessons/private-lessons[scb_private_lesson]
Summer Camps/summer-camps[scb_summer_camp]
Booking Status/booking-status[scb_booking_status]
My Bookings/my-bookings[scb_my_bookings]
ℹ️

You can also create pages manually and paste shortcodes yourself. Each shortcode is a self-contained booking widget.

Step 3 – Add Coaches

Go to Squash Club → Coaches to edit or add your coaching staff. For each coach you can set:

  • Name, photo, bio, and specialties
  • Hourly rate & semi-private rate
  • Saturday availability schedule (working hours, lunch break, day-off)
  • Active / Inactive status

Step 4 – Set Up Clinic Slots

Go to Squash Club → Sunday Clinics and click "Generate 4 Weeks" to auto-create the next month of clinic sessions based on your configured times and capacities.

Step 5 – Add Menus & Go Live

Add the booking pages to your WordPress navigation menus via Appearance → Menus, then your site is ready for bookings!

🗓️ Sunday Squash Clinics

Clinics are weekly group training sessions held every Sunday. The system supports both Junior and Adult categories with separate time slots and pricing.

Default Schedule

TimeDurationCategoryDefault Price
9:00 AM – 10:00 AM60 minJunior$25
10:00 AM – 11:00 AM60 minJunior$25
11:00 AM – 12:00 PM60 minJunior$25
12:00 PM – 1:00 PM60 minAdult$30

Key Features

  • Capacity Control – Default 15 players per slot. Fully configurable per slot.
  • Atomic Booking Counter – Prevents double-booking under concurrent requests.
  • Automatic Slot Generation – "Generate 4 Weeks" button or weekly cron auto-fills the schedule.
  • Booking Window – Configurable: opens X days before slot, closes Y hours before it starts.
  • Waitlist – When full, customers can join the waitlist. They are promoted automatically when a spot opens.

  • Open an attendee list modal per slot
  • Add, edit, or delete individual slots
  • Set slot status: open, full, cancelled
  • Generate the next 4 weeks of slots with one click

🎾 Private & Semi-Private Lessons

Saturday coaching sessions with certified squash professionals. Customers choose a coach, pick an available time slot in real time, and complete payment via WooCommerce.

Lesson Types

TypePlayersDefault PriceDefault Duration
Private Lesson1 player (1-on-1)$8060 min
Semi-Private Lesson2 players$40 each60 min

Booking Process

  1. Customer selects a coach from the card gallery (photo, bio, specialties, rates shown).
  2. Customer picks a date (Saturdays only by default).
  3. Available time slots load in real time via the REST API.
  4. Customer chooses Private or Semi-Private, fills in details.
  5. Redirected to WooCommerce checkout to pay.
  6. Booking confirmed and confirmation email sent.

Conflict Prevention

The system automatically:

  • Prevents double-booking the same coach at overlapping times
  • Respects each coach's configured working hours and lunch breaks
  • Enforces a minimum advance booking buffer (default 24 hours)

Admin Management

Under Squash Club → Private Lessons you can view the weekly schedule as a timeline, see coach assignments, manage individual bookings (approve / reject / reschedule / cancel).

☀️ Summer Camp Registration

Multi-day intensive squash academies for youth players from beginner to elite competitive levels.

Camp Features

  • Age Enforcement – Each camp has configurable min_age and max_age. Registrations outside this range are rejected.
  • Registration Deadline – Registrations close at the configured deadline date.
  • Capacity Cap – Spots are reserved atomically and released if payment is not completed within the expiry window (default 30 min).
  • Parent / Guardian Info – Full parent name, email, phone, emergency contact, and relationship fields.
  • Medical & Equipment Notes – Free-text fields for health conditions and equipment requirements.
  • Liability Waiver – Digital acknowledgement checkbox before registration completes.

Admin Management

Under Squash Club → Summer Camps you can create, edit, and publish camp programs. Under Camp Registrations you can view participant rosters, parent contacts, and payment statuses.

📋 Waitlist System

When a clinic slot reaches full capacity, the waitlist system allows customers to queue up and be promoted automatically when a spot becomes available.

How It Works

  1. Slot reaches full capacity (booked_count = capacity).
  2. Booking widget displays a "Join Waitlist" option instead of "Book Now".
  3. Customer joins the waitlist — they receive a confirmation email with their queue position (e.g., Position #3).
  4. When a cancellation occurs, the system automatically promotes the next customer on the waitlist.
  5. Promoted customer receives an email alert and has a configured window (default 24 hours) to complete payment.
  6. If the promoted customer does not pay in time, their spot is released to the next person in line.

Admin Waitlist Tools

Under Squash Club → Waitlist Management:

  • View real-time queue position for each slot
  • 1-click "Promote to Booking" – Manually promote any waitlist entry
  • Remove waitlist entries manually
  • See promotion timestamps and status
ℹ️

Waitlist promotion expiry is configurable. Go to Settings → Waitlist and set the number of hours a promoted customer has to pay before the slot is offered to the next person.

📄 Page Setup Guide

Creating Pages Manually

If you prefer to set up pages yourself instead of using the auto-create feature:

  1. Go to Pages → Add New in WordPress Admin.
  2. Give the page a title (e.g., "Sunday Clinics").
  3. In the block editor, add a Shortcode block and paste the shortcode.
  4. Publish the page.
  5. Go to Squash Club → Settings → Shortcode Pages and link this page so the plugin knows its location.
⚠️

The [scb_my_bookings] shortcode requires the user to be logged in. Consider restricting that page to logged-in users using a membership plugin or WooCommerce's account functionality.

Recommended Theme Compatibility

The plugin's frontend widgets are self-styled with scoped CSS. They work with any WordPress theme that uses standard page templates. For best results, use a full-width page template (no sidebar) for booking pages.

🔄 Customer Booking Flow

Clinic / Lesson / Camp Booking

  1. Visit page – Customer navigates to the booking page on your website.
  2. Select service – Chooses a slot, coach, or camp program.
  3. Fill details – Enters name, email, phone, skill level, and any service-specific fields.
  4. Submit form – A WooCommerce order is created automatically in the background.
  5. Checkout redirect – Customer is taken to the WooCommerce payment page to pay.
  6. Payment – Customer completes payment via any active WooCommerce gateway (Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
  7. Confirmation – Booking status updates to paid / approved. A branded HTML confirmation email is sent to the customer.
  8. Admin alert – Club management receives an email notification of the new booking.

Booking Reference Numbers

Every booking receives a unique reference in the format:

  • Clinic: SCB-CLN-20261025-WT2M
  • Lesson: SCB-LES-20261024-376Y
  • Camp: SCB-CMP-20261019-GI0T

Customers can use this reference on the Booking Status page to track their booking at any time.

Unpaid Booking Expiry

If a customer abandons checkout without paying, the reserved capacity is automatically released after the configured timeout (default 60 minutes). The WooCommerce order is left in pending status.

🛒 WooCommerce Integration

How Orders Are Created

When a customer submits a booking form, the plugin programmatically creates a WooCommerce order with:

  • Correct item name (e.g., "Sunday Junior Clinic – Nov 10, 2026 9:00 AM")
  • Correct price and currency
  • Customer billing info pre-filled from their submitted details
  • Order meta containing the booking reference and service details

The customer is then redirected to the WooCommerce checkout payment page to pay using any active payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, bank transfer, etc.).

Bidirectional Status Sync

WooCommerce Order StatusBooking Status Update
completed or processingBooking → paid + approved
cancelled or failedCapacity released back to slot / camp
refundedPayment status → refunded, audit log updated

Recommended Payment Gateways

  • Stripe for WooCommerce (official plugin) – Credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • PayPal Payments – PayPal, Pay Later
  • WooCommerce Payments – Stripe-powered, built into WooCommerce
  • Cheque / Bank Transfer – Free gateways bundled with WooCommerce (manual payment)
✅

No additional configuration is needed in the plugin for payment gateways — just activate your preferred gateway in WooCommerce → Settings → Payments and it will automatically be available at checkout.

⏱️ Automated Cron Tasks

The plugin registers three background tasks with WordPress Cron (WP-Cron):

TaskFrequencyWhat It Does
scb_weekly_clinic_generation Weekly Auto-generates the next 4 weeks of Sunday clinic slots based on the configured schedule
scb_cleanup_abandoned_checkouts Hourly Releases reserved capacity from bookings where the customer never completed payment
scb_check_waitlist_expiry Hourly Checks promoted waitlist entries that exceeded payment deadline and offers the spot to the next player
⚠️

WP-Cron relies on website traffic to trigger. For high-reliability production use, disable WP-Cron in wp-config.php and set up a real server cron job: */15 * * * * wget -q -O- https://yourdomain.com/wp-cron.php?doing_wp_cron

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can customers book without creating a WordPress account?

No. All booking submissions require the customer to be logged in. The [scb_my_bookings] portal and booking forms use WordPress authentication. Customers must have a WordPress account (or WooCommerce customer account) to book.

Can I accept payment methods other than credit card?

Yes — any WooCommerce payment gateway will work. This includes bank transfer, cheque, Stripe, PayPal, Square, and hundreds of others available in the WooCommerce marketplace. Simply install and activate your preferred gateway.

What happens if a customer books but never pays?

The booking is created in pending status and a WooCommerce order is created. If payment is not completed within the configured timeout (default 60 minutes), the hourly cron job automatically releases the reserved capacity back to the slot. The WooCommerce order remains as a record.

Can I offer refunds through the plugin?

Refunds are processed through WooCommerce — either via the payment gateway's refund feature or manually in WooCommerce → Orders. When a WooCommerce order is refunded, the plugin automatically updates the payment status to refunded in the audit log.

How do I add more clinic time slots or change the schedule?

Go to Squash Club → Settings and update the clinic_slots array with your desired times and categories. Then go to Sunday Clinics → Generate 4 Weeks to generate new slots on the updated schedule. Alternatively, add individual slots manually from the Sunday Clinics view.

Can I limit the clinic booking widget to show only junior sessions?

Yes. Use the shortcode attribute: [scb_sunday_clinic category="junior"] for junior-only or [scb_sunday_clinic category="adult"] for adult-only. Using [scb_sunday_clinic] without an attribute shows all categories.

How do I export a list of all bookings?

Go to Squash Club → Reports & Exports and use the CSV Export buttons for Bookings, Camp Registrations, or Customers. The exported file is formatted for use in Excel or Google Sheets.

Can I use the plugin on a multisite WordPress installation?

The plugin is designed for standard single-site installations. Multisite support (network activation) is not officially supported in the current version. Each site in a multisite network would need its own independent activation.

How do I completely remove the plugin and all its data?

Go to Squash Club → Settings → Reset Tables to delete all 12 plugin database tables and their data. Then deactivate and delete the plugin from WP Admin → Plugins. The uninstall routine is also called automatically when you delete the plugin via the WordPress admin.
